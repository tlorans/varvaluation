# Ang–Liu walkthrough resources

## Knowledge

- [Hamilton, J. D. (1994). *Time Series Analysis*. Princeton University Press](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691042893/time-series-analysis)
  The AR(1) with intercept: \(y_t = c + \phi y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t\). If \(|\phi|<1\), the process has a constant mean \(c/(1-\phi)\). Use for: why 6% is the rest point of \(0.03 + 0.50\lambda\).
- [Mean reversion in an AR(1) (Cross Validated)](https://stats.stackexchange.com/questions/108391/mean-reversion-in-ar1-process)
  \(x_t = a + b x_{t-1}\) has mean \(a/(1-b)\). Use for: the one-line check \(0.03/(1-0.50)=0.06\).
- [Ang and Liu (2004), “How to discount cashflows with time-varying expected returns,” *Journal of Finance*](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2004.00710.x)
  The paper this repo implements. Use later: product identity, quadratic \(\mu\), the curve \(\mu_t(n)\). Not for lesson 1.
- [Handbook: Core idea](https://tlorans.github.io/varvaluation/guide/core/)
  Value is \(E[\text{discount path}\times\text{cash flow}]\). Use later: why next year’s premium matters for a price.
