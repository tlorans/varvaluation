# A numerical walkthrough

This is a course, not one page. One idea per lesson. Do not skip ahead.

You are valuing a claim that pays cash once a year. The return you require this year is 3% plus the **equity premium**. Until you can compute *next year’s* premium from this year’s, you cannot price anything.

1. **Next year’s premium** — one number, one rule. [Open the lesson](../lessons/0001-next-years-premium.html).
2. The world \(X=(g,\lambda)\) — a VAR. Not yet.
3. Next year’s cash. Not yet.
4. This year’s discount. Not yet.
5. Two years. Not yet.
6. The curve. Not yet.

The numbers in the lessons are made up. Nothing is estimated from returns. The paper with six variables is [Ang and Liu (2004)](angliu.md); it waits until this course is done.

Ask after lesson 1 if the 6% case or the 3% case is still muddy.
