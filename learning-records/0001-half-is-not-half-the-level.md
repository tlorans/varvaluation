# The 0.50 is a weight on this year’s premium, not “keep half”

The sentence “0.50 is stickiness: half of this year’s premium carries into next year” was read as next year = 0.50 × this year. That would send 6% to 3%. The user was right to reject it.

Future lessons must show the full rule `0.03 + 0.50 × this year’s premium` first, then plug in 6% and 3% as two separate calculations. Do not mention growth until that arithmetic is used correctly.
