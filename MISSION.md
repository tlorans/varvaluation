# Mission: Follow Ang and Liu on one claim

## Why
You are writing and reading the varvaluation handbook. You cannot check the code, the curve, or the December 2000 picture until you can compute next year’s required return from this year’s premium. That is the bottleneck.

## Success looks like
- Given this year’s equity premium, you can compute next year’s expected premium by hand
- You can say why 6% stays 6% and why 3% becomes 4.5% without mixing in growth
- Later: you can walk the same rule on a pair \((g,\lambda)\) and then on a strip of cash

## Constraints
- One idea per lesson. Do not jump from an AR to a VAR in the same paragraph
- Name the noun every time (the *premium* is 3%, not “you are at 3%”)
- Numpy only after the arithmetic is on the page
- Working memory is small; a lesson must be finishable in minutes

## Out of scope

- The six-variable paper state
