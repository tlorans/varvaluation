# Teaching notes

- The user found the handbook and the first walkthrough hard because too many objects were named before they appeared.
- Do not open with a glossary.
- Do not argue the whole curve in the setup.
- “Stickiness 0.50” was read as “next year = half this year.” That would turn 6% into 3%. The 0.50 is the weight on *this year’s premium* in `0.03 + 0.50 λ`.
- Never say “you are at 3%.” Say “the premium is 3%.”
- Damodaran-style story is welcome only if it stays on one object.
- Prefer a table of this-year → next-year over prose about “gaps.”
- Charts must be generated from the numpy the reader just saw.
